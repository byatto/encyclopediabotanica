Encyclopedia Botanica

    Encyclopedia Botanica

    A personal, offline-first houseplant care catalogue. Reference data (family, care requirements, pests, origins) lives in js/plants.js and is the same on every device. Anything you fill in here — acquired dates, locations, notes — is saved only on this device.

    Install

    On Android Chrome: tap the ⋮ menu → Add to Home screen / Install app. Once installed it works fully offline.

    Your data

    This browser isn't persisting saved data (private/incognito mode, or storage is disabled) — anything you log will be lost when you close the app.

    Care-log notes, photos you set on a plant, and any plants you've added with + Add plant all live only on this device — nothing is ever uploaded anywhere. When you first add a photo, the app also asks the browser to protect this data from automatic cleanup (no prompt to you either way — some browsers grant it silently). Export a backup occasionally, especially before reinstalling or switching phones — photos make the backup file a few MB, which is expected.

    Print / PDF

    Need paper copies or a PDF of the full catalogue? Use the print view — same data, A4 layout, including your on-device photos and locally-added plants.

    Adding a plant

    Tap + Add plant on the list screen. It walks you through an optional photo, naming the plant, copying a ready-made prompt to paste into any AI assistant, and pasting its reply back in — the app checks the reply over and adds it straight to your catalogue on this device.

    A plant added this way is marked Local until you publish it: use Export plants.js below to download a copy of js/plants.js with your local plants folded in, ready to commit to the repo. Once committed, the app quietly stops using its local copy in favour of the real one — nothing is deleted, so it's always safe to try.
